ABOUT US

ClubSport Goodwill (CSG) brings together automotive enthusiasts through a shared passion for cars, community engagement and making a positive difference.

WHO WE ARE

ClubSport Goodwill is an automotive community dedicated to bringing people together through their love of cars to support charitable causes trough events and fundraising activities.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to use automotive passion to create positive change, momorable automotive experiences, encourage generosity and support meaningful charitable initiatives.

OUR VISION

We aim to build a community where automotive enthusiasm creates lasting positive impact.

While supercars are an important part of the experience, the central purpose of CSG is to use the excitement and passion surrounding these vehicles to create positive social impact.

OUR VALUES

  • Community: Bringing automotive enthusiasts together.
  • Generosity: Encouraging meaningful charitable giving.
  • Transparency: Clearly communicating how funds are raised and used.
  • Experience: Creating memorable events for supporters.
  • Responsibility: Using the supercar community to support worthwhile causes.
